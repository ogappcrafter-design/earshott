# Play Console listing: Earshot

**App name (30 max):** Earshot: Voice Amplifier
**Short description (80 max):** Amplify, lock onto a voice, record and transcribe. Private and on-device.

**Full description:**
Hear the voice you came for.

Earshot turns your phone and earbuds into a personal sound amplifier that knows who you're trying to hear. Teach it a voice with a 20-second sample, and Focus mode lifts that voice while background noise falls away.

- Live amplification with a 10-band equalizer and presets for noisy rooms, lectures and TV
- Personal ear curve: a two-minute tone check tunes the EQ to your hearing
- Voice Focus: save up to five voices and switch between them
- Adaptive noise reduction and a loudness ceiling that tames sudden bangs
- Studio-quality recordings (24-bit WAV) with bookmarks
- Automatic transcripts made on your phone, searchable and timestamped
- Share or save audio and transcripts anywhere
- No account, no ads, no cloud: your audio never leaves your phone

Use wired or USB-C earbuds for real-time listening. Earshot is a personal sound amplifier, not a medical device or hearing aid.
