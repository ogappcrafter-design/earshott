# Earshot Privacy Policy
_Last updated: September 23, 2026. Developer: Outbox Enter._

**Short version: your audio stays on your phone.**

**Microphone.** Earshot uses the microphone only while you are listening, recording, or recording a voice sample. It does not listen in the background after you leave the app.

**Recordings, voice profiles and transcripts** are stored only on your device. Transcription runs on your device. Earshot has no accounts, servers, analytics, or ads, and does not sell or share data.

**Voice profiles** store a pitch range (three numbers) and a name. The sample audio used to create them is discarded.

**Network use.** The first transcription downloads a speech-recognition model from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). No audio or text is sent. That request is subject to Hugging Face's privacy policy.

**Sharing.** Files leave your phone only when you tap Share or Save and choose a destination.

**Deleting data.** Delete recordings or voices in the app, or uninstall Earshot to remove everything.

**Children.** Earshot is not directed at children under 13.

**Recording others.** Laws on recording conversations vary by location. You are responsible for getting any consent your local law requires.
